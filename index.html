📜 Historical Genesis & Growth

A Journey of Innovation & Academic Excellence

January 2016

Inauguration of the Faculty

Formally established through Gazette Extraordinary, becoming the premier and most modern engineering faculty in Sri Lanka to address next-generation technological needs.

2018 - 2021

Mattegoda Permanent Complex Development

Construction and commissioning of the state-of-the-art permanent engineering complex in Mattegoda, featuring high-bay structural halls, clean rooms, and computing clusters.

2022 - 2026

IESL Recognition & National Honors

Curricula aligned with IESL standards and SLQF Level 8. FOE scholars clinched Gold at the National AI Awards 2026, podium victories at MicroMaze 3.0 robotics, and NBQSA ICT trophies.

👁️

Faculty Vision

To be a centre of excellence in higher education and research in engineering, empowering innovators who serve society through technological ingenuity and ethical leadership.

🎯

Faculty Mission

Produce engineering graduates with the knowledge and aspiration to become competent engineers in conventional and emerging spheres by offering a flexible curriculum meeting global standards.
